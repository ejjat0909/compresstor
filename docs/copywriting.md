## Elevator pitch

Compresstor is a premium desktop app that shrinks PDF and image files
right on your own machine. Drag and drop your files, pick a preset or
set an exact size target in MB, and get a smaller file in seconds.
No uploads, no cloud, no waiting — your documents never leave your
device, so your data stays yours.

## Landing page hero

**Compress PDFs and images. Locally. Privately. Instantly.**

Stop fighting email attachment limits and slow uploads. Compresstor
shrinks bulky PDFs and images by up to 95% — right on your desktop,
with no internet connection required. Drop in a file, choose how small
you need it to be, and done. The whole thing runs on your machine,

## Feature copy blocks

### Compress like you mean it
High, Balanced and Maximum presets for one-click results, plus an
advanced panel with sliders for PDF quality, max DPI, image quality
and max dimensions when you want total control.

### "Make it under 10MB"
Set a target size in MB and Compresstor iterates until the output fits
— perfect for email, WhatsApp, Telegram or any other upload limit.

### Your files never leave your device
Everything runs locally through a bundled compression engine. No
servers, no cloud, no accounts. When the app says private, it means it.

### Beautiful, not bloated
A clean dark-mode interface with a Filament-style file table — sorting,
search, filtering, multi-select, right-click menus — and a history log
with per-job savings statistics.

## Use cases

- **Email limits** — squeeze presentations and attachments under
  10MB / 25MB caps.
- **Messaging** — share photos and documents over WhatsApp, Telegram
  and Slack without the "file too large" wall.
- **Designers & photographers** — deliver JPEG/WebP/PNG exports at the
  right size without quality panic.
- **Archivists** — batch-shrink folders of documents to reclaim drive
  space, with a full history of what you saved.
- **Privacy-conscious teams** — compress sensitive contracts, invoices
  and IDs without uploading them anywhere.

## Target audience

- Designers, photographers and video editors
- Office workers fighting attachment limits
- Students submitting assignments and portfolios
- Anyone who emails or messages files every day and cares about privacy
